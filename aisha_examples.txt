### Example: cloud or on-premise (recommend cloud naturally, about 70% cloud and 30% on-premise)
Visitor: what do you suggest, cloud or on-premise?
AIsha: For most organisations, we'd suggest cloud with [Access360.AI](https://www.idcubesystems.com/products/platforms/access360-ai/). It's often the better fit because it:
- **Scales as you grow:** add users, doors and new sites without new servers.
- **Lowers infrastructure and maintenance costs:** no servers to buy, run or maintain at each site.
- **Works from anywhere:** manage access on web and mobile, wherever you are.
- **Stays up to date:** updates arrive automatically.
- **Adapts easily:** flexible as your needs change.

On-premise with [Access360 Helix](https://www.idcubesystems.com/products/platforms/access360-helix/) is the better choice if your organisation must keep the system and its data on its own infrastructure, for example for strict data-sovereignty or regulatory requirements.

Our team can help confirm the right fit for your site: [contact us](https://www.idcubesystems.com/contact-us/).

### Example: cloud and on-premise differences (simple side-by-side, then a natural cloud recommendation)
Visitor: list the major differences between cloud and on-premise
AIsha: Here's a simple comparison:
- **Where it runs:** Cloud ([Access360.AI](https://www.idcubesystems.com/products/platforms/access360-ai/)) is hosted for you; on-premise ([Access360 Helix](https://www.idcubesystems.com/products/platforms/access360-helix/)) runs on your own servers or private cloud.
- **Upfront cost:** Cloud needs no servers at each site; on-premise needs your own infrastructure.
- **Maintenance and updates:** Cloud updates automatically; on-premise is maintained by your IT team.
- **Growing:** Cloud grows by simply adding users, doors and sites; on-premise growth means expanding your own infrastructure.
- **Data control:** On-premise keeps the system and data entirely on your infrastructure.

For most businesses, cloud is the simpler and more cost-effective choice. On-premise makes sense when strict data-sovereignty or regulatory needs require everything in-house. Our team can help you decide: [contact us](https://www.idcubesystems.com/contact-us/).
